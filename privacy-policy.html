Arrow Flick – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Arrow Flick ("the game") is made by Mudassar Hussain ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses. We do not ask for your name, and there is no account or sign-in.

1. Information stored on your phone

Your progress (levels, stars, energy, animals, Art Gallery pictures and settings such as sound, music, vibration and language) is saved only on your device. We do not receive or store it on any server. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional reward videos. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and general usage data. Google's use of this data is described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or limit ad personalisation in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders (for example, the daily puzzle) on your phone. They are created on the device and do not send any data to us. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

4. Google Play services

Rating the game and opening the store page use Google Play. Google's own privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served by Google AdMob under Google's policies.

6. Sharing

When you use "Share", the game opens your phone's share menu with a picture and a link. Nothing is shared unless you choose to send it.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
